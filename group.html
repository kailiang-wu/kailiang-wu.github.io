Research Group

Intelligent Computing–Astrophysics Nexus (ICAN)

We work on numerical methods, scientific machine learning, computational fluid dynamics, and astrophysics.

Mentoring summary at Southern University of Science and Technology (since 2021): 3 research assistant professors (RAPs), 11 postdoctoral fellows, 18 graduate students, and 68 undergraduate students; multiple mentees have secured tenure-track assistant or associate professor positions.
